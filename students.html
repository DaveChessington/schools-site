Easily browse, register, and manage students in our academic system. This platform ensures simple and efficient access to student records.

Search for a Student

The Search section enables users to quickly find a specific student by name. Enter a full or partial name to retrieve key details about any registered student in the system.

Student Management

This section provides an overview of all registered students, showing their essential information such as name, email, and course. Administrators can use this interface to view, edit, or remove records with ease, maintaining an organized student database.

Register New Student

Add new students by entering their personal and academic details. This section streamlines enrollment and ensures accurate database records.
